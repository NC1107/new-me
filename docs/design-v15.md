# New Me v15: fewer taps to the thing you came for

Earlier design documents still hold unless a section below changes them.

## Why

Nick found two places where the answer sat one layer too deep.
Learning an exercise took Train, Exercises, the exercise, then a "How to do it" sheet.
The Food tab could only log a meal; saving a food meant going through My foods first.

## The exercise page shows its how-to

- The exercise page shows the start and end photos, the numbered steps and the form cues in a "How to do it" card, below the best set.
- The "How to do it" button and its sheet are gone from that page.
- The info button on each row of the exercise list still opens the sheet, for a look without leaving the list.
- A session card keeps its info button, since the session page is about logging sets and the sheet closes back to it.
- Custom exercises have no how-to, so their page has no such card.

## One Add button on the Food tab

- "Log a meal" became "Add", which asks "What are you adding?" with two choices.
- Meal opens the meal form with the search focused, as before.
- Food opens New food directly.
- New food starts with a search box with barcode scanning; picking a result swaps the page for that food filled in, so back still returns to the tab.
- A new food says "Saved <name> to My foods" when it is saved.

## Smaller fixes

- Open Food Facts brand lists such as "Nutella, Ferrero" no longer show a double space, and a brand the name already says is dropped when another brand remains.
- A saved food that was never logged leaves out "used 0 times".
- The exercise list's dividers are 2 px like every other card.

## Reviewed and left alone

- Today's quest plus buttons open their log form directly.
- The Me tab rows are settings pages one level deep.
- The readiness sheet replaces itself with the plan sheet instead of stacking.
